How coordination works

Request → match → accept → arrive
  1. 1

    Raise a request

    App, SMS or voice call. Emergency type and village are captured first — no long forms.

  2. 2

    Match nearby drivers

    Rules-based matching alerts the nearest suitable verified vehicles — not a single vehicle lottery.

  3. 3

    Driver accepts

    The patient and the ASHA/health worker see who is coming, the vehicle and a live ETA.

  4. 4

    Escalate if unanswered

    If nobody accepts quickly, the app pushes the user to call 108/112 instead of waiting.

How the match is scored

A transparent rules-based score — no black-box AI. The prototype ranks by distance, vehicle suitability for the emergency type, and driver availability.

Distanceweight 45%
Vehicle suitabilityweight 35%
Availability & verificationweight 20%

Vehicle suitability matters. A Toto or e-rickshaw is not appropriate for critical trauma. The matcher prefers a stretcher-capable vehicle for accidents and pregnancy cases.

Verification in this prototype is illustrative. A real pilot needs licence, RC, insurance, police verification and a signed code of conduct before a driver can receive patient requests.

SMS simulator

Works on any phone with GSM coverage and no data plan. A short structured alert is sent to the three closest verified drivers; the first to reply YES is assigned.

Alert template

GRAM-RIDE ALERT | Medical | Rampur Kalan | near Govt. Primary School | Patient stable, needs transport to PHC Rampur (2.1 km). Reply YES to accept. Call 108 for critical care.

  • Hindi + local-language templates with the caller's village and landmark.
  • No smartphone, app install or data pack required.
  • SMS delivery is not guaranteed; escalation to a voice call is required if no reply arrives in 3 minutes.

Safety & scope

Built to complement 112, 108 and 102 — not replace them

Gram-Emergency Ride is a transport-coordination concept, not a medical service. It does not provide clinical care, and it cannot guarantee a vehicle.

Official services first

For life-threatening emergencies call 112 or 108 immediately. 102 supports patient transport, particularly for pregnant women and children.

Call 112 Call 108 Call 102

Verification & consent

Drivers must opt in and be verified. Location is shared per request, with the caller's consent, and only with the drivers being alerted.

Escalation, not waiting

If no driver accepts in a short window, the app tells the user to call 108/112 and can alert an authorised emergency contact.

Targets are not results

Any impact figures — e.g. faster median time to transport — are pilot targets to be measured against a documented baseline, not claims of proven outcomes.

Prototype disclosure: drivers, ETAs, GPS coordinates and facility availability on this page are fictional demo data. No request is transmitted to any real driver or dispatch centre.
